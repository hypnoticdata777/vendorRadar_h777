# VendorRadar

VendorRadar is in early discovery. This repository currently preserves public-safe product research and reusable research tooling for a vendor intelligence concept in property management.

## Repository Structure

- `docs/research/` - cleaned research dossiers and demand-mining synthesis.
- `tools/research-workbook/` - reusable browser-based workbook for future competitor teardown and demand-mining loops.

## Public-Safe Research Policy

Committed research artifacts should be synthesized and developer-friendly. Do not commit raw signup emails, raw screenshots, raw Reddit dumps, usernames, identifying anecdotes, long verbatim excerpts, sensitive pricing strategy, or redundant DOCX/PDF binaries when a cleaned Markdown source exists.
