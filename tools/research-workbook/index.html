How to Use This Research Loop

Use one copy of this workbook per product, competitor, or demand-mining batch. Write generously, classify every important claim, and keep unknowns visible until they are confirmed or rejected.

1
Teardown what exists. Capture live evidence, public claims, workflows, pricing gates, UX defects, data models, moats, and the lessons worth stealing or avoiding.
2
Mine demand. Read postings, communities, support threads, and buyer language. Look for repeated paid pain, not just interesting complaints.
3
Generate the dossier only when ready. Reconcile contradictions, inventory evidence, list unknowns, then open the gate for a polished research artifact.

Simple rhythm

  • Start with the setup block so exports have context.
  • Add numbered findings as you go, even rough ones.
  • Use evidence labels honestly: confirmed, claim, inference, unknown.
  • When the same pain appears repeatedly in demand mining, turn it into a requirement.
  • Do not generate the dossier until the readiness gate is green.
